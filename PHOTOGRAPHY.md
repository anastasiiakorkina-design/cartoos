# Photography shot list — Cartoos launch shoot

Every image slot on the site currently shows an **in-brand generative
placeholder with a visible label** (never stock). Each label matches a shot
below. To go live, replace each `<Photo>` component's `<Plate>` with the
delivered asset via `next/image` and remove the `marked` label.

**Direction:** dark grounds, natural window light, warm highlights. Stone,
wood, fire, smoke, olive branches, fresh herbs. Macro over wide. Shoot
during a real service where possible — hands, steam, movement. No
white-background product shots.
